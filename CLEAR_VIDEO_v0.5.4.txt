v0.5.4 targets the two remaining visible artifacts from v0.5.3:

1. "Images over each other": switched from weave deinterlacing to BOB. A complete
   top or bottom field is displayed by doubling its lines, so frames from two
   different instants are never mixed.

2. Heavy red color: SAA7113 is now explicitly configured for NTSC-M/525-60 first,
   matching the 720x480 stream that became stable in v0.5.3. Output remains YUYV as
   Linux em28xx expects. A visible COLOR MODE button cycles AUTO/YUYV/YVYU/UYVY/VYUY
   in the same APK, so a remaining chroma-order issue can be corrected without a new build.

The native ISO queue remains continuous. Queue size is slightly reduced to improve
startup reliability after the errno=12 starts seen in v0.5.3.
