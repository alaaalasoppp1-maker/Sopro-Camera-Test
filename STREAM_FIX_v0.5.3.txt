v0.5.3 fixes the severe tearing/corruption seen in v0.5.2.

- Uses 8 continuously queued ISO URBs instead of one-at-a-time URBs.
- Resubmits each URB immediately after reaping to avoid dropped chunks.
- Restores EM28xx VINMODE 0x10 (CbYCrY) for the external SAA7113 decoder.
- Selects the first ISO alt meeting Linux em28xx bandwidth requirement: 2888 B/microframe; on this camera alt object 6 = 2892.
- Drops fields below 90% completeness instead of rendering corrupted blocks.
- Prevents stale TOP/BOTTOM fields from being paired across frames.
