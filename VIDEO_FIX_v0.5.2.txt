v0.5.2 fixes the no-preview bug.

v0.5.1 proved that the camera, EM2820 bridge, SAA7113 and ISO endpoint were alive,
but it selected video alternate setting 1, which only provides 1024 bytes per
microframe. Full analog video needs much more bandwidth.

This version decodes high-bandwidth wMaxPacketSize correctly and selects the
highest-bandwidth alternate setting. On this camera raw 0x1400 decodes to
1024 * 3 = 3072 bytes per microframe.

It also configures EM28xx capture geometry explicitly and tries PAL 720x576
and NTSC 720x480 in the same APK. Frames are rendered only after a valid
top+bottom field pair is assembled.
